Curious... very, very curious. Look closely at what you are holding, my dear. The wood is Chestnut. Chestnut wands are famously select; they are drawn to those who are skilled tamers of magical beasts, those who possess great botanical flair, and those who are deeply, inherently kind and thoughtful. It requires a master who is grounded, yet endlessly imaginative. And woven into its core is a Phoenix feather—capable of the greatest range of magic, though it may take its own time to reveal its full depth. Phoenix feathers are fiercely independent, yet they respond profoundly to those with a pure heart and a protective instinct for their friends.   As for its craftsmanship—wrapped in natural vines and adorned with bright, berry-like ruby droplets—this is a wand that mirrors your very soul. It tells the story of someone who finds immense happiness in the small, beautiful details of the world, who nurtures life, and who stands unshakeably by those she loves. Treat this wand with gentleness and courage, and there is no magic in this world you cannot touch.
